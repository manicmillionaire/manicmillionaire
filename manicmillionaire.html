At various points I've been a millionaire, a workaholic, a fitness fanatic, a reckless optimist, a broken mess — and everything in between.

The truth is that success doesn't arrive with instructions. Nobody tells you what happens when the money comes but the questions remain.

I've survived suicide attempts and spells in mental hospitals. I'm still here, still building. That's the part most success stories quietly leave out.

Manic Millionaire is my attempt to document the journey.

01

My Story

From a military boarding school kid to building online businesses, buying and selling digital assets, and searching for a life that actually feels meaningful.

I've also been to the bottom — suicide attempts, time in mental hospitals — and climbed back out. This isn't a highlight reel. It's the whole thing: the wins, the breakdowns, and what it actually takes to keep going.

I've given money away, chased extreme fitness goals, and stopped drinking after periods where it became an escape. One day I want to disappear to Scotland with dogs, mountain bikes, sheep and pigs.

Most "millionaire" brands are about Lamborghinis. Mine is about trying to figure out what actually matters after you make the money — and how to stay alive to enjoy it.

The journey hasn't been linear.
That's the point.
